Before They Graduate

Archive Mission

Before They Graduate is a media archive of what Nigerian undergraduates are building, learning, researching, and leading before they graduate.

I founded it as a small documentary project at FUTA. It has grown into an open-access archive of documentaries, written stories, and student submissions.

The aim is a record. Years from now, anyone asking what Nigerian undergraduates were doing in the 2020s should have something to read and watch.

Visit beforetheygraduate.app or watch on YouTube.

Support the archive

Before They Graduate runs on my own time and equipment. Sponsorship goes toward three things:

  • Equipment — camera, audio and editing hardware for documentary production.
  • Travel — reaching students and campuses beyond FUTA.
  • Hosting — keeping the archive online and accessible.

See the archive itself at beforetheygraduate.app for what's been published so far.
