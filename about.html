Background

I study Surveying and Geoinformatics at the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA), and I currently serve as President of the Nigerian Institution of Surveying and Geoinformatics Students (NISGS), FUTA Chapter.

I trained in AI and machine learning at Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Nigeria (RAIN), and I continue to explore GeoAI, GIS, remote sensing, spatial data, machine learning, and Python.

I also work in visual storytelling through VFX, video editing, and documentary production. This creative background shapes how I communicate technical ideas and preserve stories that matter.

Based in Akure, Nigeria.
