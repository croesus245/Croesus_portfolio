Undergraduate suspension in Nigeria

A research and documentary project examining how suspension policies shape student progression, wellbeing, and long-term outcomes.

I am currently gathering institutional patterns, lived student experiences, and policy context to produce both research outputs and a suspension documentary archive.

If you want to collaborate or sponsor this work, I am open, and I can share all research materials connected to the suspension documentary with partners and supporters.
