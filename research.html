Published research

MamaCare AI: A GeoAI Framework for Maternal Healthcare Accessibility Analysis in Underserved Communities

First author, solo-authored. ICML 2026 Workshop MusIML, Poster Track 1 — published June 2026.

A spatial accessibility analysis across 1,510 grid points in Kebbi State, Nigeria, integrating GRID3 facility records (1,207 facilities), WorldPop population estimates, and Nigeria DHS health indicators. DBSCAN spatial clustering isolated priority underserved zones (silhouette score 0.83), feeding a composite risk score across all 21 Local Government Areas that flagged Argungu, Augie and Dandi as critical intervention priorities. The paper proposes a phased AI-assisted mobile clinic deployment reaching 310,000 women, and estimates that roughly 62% of women of reproductive age in Kebbi State live more than 5 km from the nearest health facility.

Non-archival workshop submission, eligible for journal or full-conference extension. Read it on OpenReview · Direct PDF.

Geospatial Intelligence for Targeted Maternal Health Interventions in Northern Nigeria: A Case Study of Kebbi State

Co-authored with Onyemaachi John Nnamani. Accepted book chapter, forthcoming in Sustainable and Multidisciplinary Advanced Research Trends for AI-Driven Future in Africa, 2026.

Extends the Kebbi State work — how geospatial intelligence and AI-driven spatial analysis can support targeted maternal health interventions, improve resource allocation, and strengthen healthcare access in underserved regions.

Using AI and Machine Learning for Land Use Classification in Zimbabwe: Enhancing Geospatial Governance for SDG Achievement

Co-author. Published via ResearchGate, 2025.

Automated land-use classification from multi-temporal Landsat 8 and Sentinel-2 imagery across three governance-relevant areas in Zimbabwe, comparing four machine learning algorithms for forest, urban and agricultural monitoring in support of SDG reporting.
